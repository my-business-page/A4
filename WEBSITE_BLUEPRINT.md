## Site map
Home / Menu / Story / Order & Pickup / Reopening Updates / Bulk & Events / FAQ / Contact.

## UX goal
Build a brand site that remains useful during temporary pauses and makes reopening dates immediately visible. The top CTA changes between “Order” and “See reopening updates”.

## Visual direction
Warm family hospitality with premium café restraint: parchment, espresso, soft sage, charcoal. Use documentary-style imagery and subtle paper texture, not glossy chain-café styling.

## Motion + VFX
- Warm light sweep behind hero typography.
- Scroll reveals with staggered family/story cards.
- Menu images use parallax crop.
- WhatsApp CTA has restrained pulse only when open.
- Reopening announcement uses animated status badge.
- Page transition: paper/soft grain wipe.
- All animations reverse cleanly on scroll up.

## Functional
- Temporary closure banner system.
- Reopening schedule module.
- WhatsApp order CTA.
- Menu and pickup instructions.
- Bulk/office coffee enquiry.
- Instagram link.
- Privacy-conscious pickup location.
- Mobile-first and accessible.

## Important
Do not state that the cafe is currently open until a fresh owner/source check confirms it. Do not expose private family/residential information beyond what the business publicly chooses.
