THE STORY

A cafe that
feels like home.

“Real warmth
and proper coffee.”

The Weekenders Cafe is described publicly as a family-run HDB cafe in Ang Mo Kio — a neighbourhood place built around coffee and human connection rather than a polished chain experience.

The experience is deliberately simple: order your drink, pick it up around the home, and enjoy it outside. Local descriptions mention the lawn outside the apartment as the pickup point.

We keep the exact residential details private here. Check the live reopening and order information before visiting.
